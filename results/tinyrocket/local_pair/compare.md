## Reading rules (fixed before the numbers were seen)

- n = 1 per arm. A WL difference under ~5% or a WNS difference under ~10–15% is a direction, not a finding.
- Runtime is the solid part of the comparison.
- A is not timing-driven and B is, so a timing loss for A is expected (paper §6).
